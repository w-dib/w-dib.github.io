**Last updated: 26 July 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies to **Grubstake**, a game developed by **Walid Dib** ("we", "us"). It explains what information the game does and does not handle.

**Short version: Grubstake does not collect, store, or share your personal data.**

## Information we collect

None. Grubstake is a single-player, offline game. It does not ask for an account, a login, your name, email, location, or any personal information, and it contains no analytics or tracking.

## Game saves

Your progress and settings are stored **locally on your device** (or, in the web version, in your browser). They never leave your device, and we cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them.

## Purchases

The optional in-app purchase to unlock the full game is processed entirely by **Google Play**. We never receive or store your payment details. Google's handling of that transaction is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Advertising

Grubstake contains **no ads** and no advertising SDKs.

## Children

Grubstake is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
